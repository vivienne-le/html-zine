I want to travel. I have always wanted to travel and be autonomous of where my physical being is existing. I want to move. My first step of moving was transitioning out of my parents bedroom to my own when I was very little. I would sleep like a baby in my own sanctuary. Maybe because I was just a baby but then I grew to be just a young girl wanting to move outside of home. Away from my family, away from my friends, outside into the unknown. In Germany we have a term for this, called “fernweh”. “Fernweh” is an adjective describing the feeling of longing for being somewhere far away from home. Ever since I was little, I had always had this urge to explore what is outside of my home, outside of comfort zone, outside of my vision and outside of my perception. Ever since I was little, I always had this dream to explore and be independently doing what I love. What I love and value the most is freedom. Feeling free is so self-fulfilling to me. However, one must see acknowledge that having the choice is a real pressure. Especially at a young age, when none of my peers had figured it out either. At the same time, it can also be beautiful. Leaving the known behind to step into the new is hard and beautiful. Choose your hard to unfold the beauty. Find beauty in everything and attract it naturally. Hearing the ocean, birds chirping, the wind whistling. Seeing the sunrise unfold, seasons changing, people smiling from one ear to the other. All of those things I love so I chose to just do it. Book that trip, make those memories, have the most fun of your life while wandering around into an unknown direction.
